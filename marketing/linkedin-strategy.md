# NeoDonkey Marketing Strategy: LinkedIn

## Profile Optimization
- **Headline:** "Building the ERP that belongs to you | Headless, local-first, browser-native | EUPL-1.2"
- **About:** Tell the founder story — why NeoDonkey exists, the problem with traditional ERP, the vision for software sovereignty
- **Featured:** Link to neodonkey.github.io, GitHub repo, and key blog posts
- **Banner:** Create branded banner with tagline
