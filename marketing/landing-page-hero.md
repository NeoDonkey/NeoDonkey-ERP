You** [1, 2]

### **A headless, zero-dependency, no-server ERP designed for absolute 
structural sovereignty [1, 3, 4]. Your company data lives in a flat folder as an
append-only, cryptographically signed Git repository—zero vendor lock-in, zero 
hosting overhead, and zero databases to manage [1, 5, 6].**

---

### **Built for CTOs & Technical Founders**

*   **Git is Your Single Source of Truth:** Not "backed up to Git"—**the 
repository *is* your database** [7]. Every invoice, stock change, and business 
fact is written directly to files as an atomic, cryptographically signed commit 
[1, 2, 7]. Because there are no proprietary formats, your data is never held 
hostage, and your exit path is as simple as running `cd` into your company 
folder [5, 8].
*   **A No-Server, Zero-Dependency Architecture:** NeoDonkey is **headless by 
construction** [7]. The core contains plain ES modules, no framework, and **zero
dependencies in `package.json`** [2, 3]. Built strictly on standard, long-lived 
browser APIs like `crypto.subtle`, `CompressionStream`, and 
`FileSystemDirectoryHandle`, it executes entirely in your browser with zero 
server infrastructure to deploy or scale [2, 3].
*   **Cryptographic, Local-First Security:** Logging in instantly generates a 
non-extractable Ed25519 key pair directly on your machine [2, 9]. Your operating
model's authority is verified mathematically through cryptographic commit 
signatures rather than vulnerable access control tables—meaning **nobody can 
alter your software or access your ledger without your key** [7, 10].
