# Category manager

Owns a slice of the assortment — nuts, or dried fruit, or chocolate — commercially. Decides what we
list, what we delist, what it costs the customer, and what the retail conditions are. This is the role
whose decisions show up in the weekly margin review, and the role that has to answer for them.

Two authorities matter. **Articles:** this role brings an article to `active`, and that is a gate
rather than a formality — an article without a complete allergen declaration and a nutrition table
cannot go live, and the rule refuses it by name. **Retail conditions:** the annual percentage a
grocery chain negotiates, up to the same ten percent boundary that applies to any discount.

## Purpose

- Own the assortment for their categories: listing, delisting, clearance.
- Complete the article master: allergens, nutrition, origin, GTIN, shelf life, batch obligation.
- Negotiate and record retail conditions per customer.
- Plan clearance actions for near-expiry stock before it becomes a write-off.
- Bring the category numbers to the weekly margin review.

## Notes

### Authorised for
`processes/article-onboarding.md`, `processes/b2b-retail-order.md`, `processes/discount-posting.md`

### Not authorised for
A discount above ten percent — that is `processes/discount-approval.md` and the managing director.
Anything about batches, quality or stock movement. Issuing invoices or credit notes.

### Reports to
`managing-director`

### Sees
Articles, costs, margins, stock levels, sales orders, discounts and customers in their categories.
Supplier terms for the articles they own. Not payments, and not the inspection detail behind a blocked
batch — they see that it was blocked.
