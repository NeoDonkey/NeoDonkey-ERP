# Customer service agent

Answers the customer. In a business where sixty percent of revenue comes from consumers, this is the
role that decides whether somebody orders again — and the role most often crippled by a system that
does not let them fix anything.

So the authority here is real. An agent may grant up to ten percent, issue a goodwill credit up to
100 EUR, accept a return, re-send a parcel. They do not need to ask. Above those numbers they do, and
the numbers are written down where everybody can see them rather than encoded in a permission nobody
can find.

The one thing an agent must not do is judge a food complaint. A customer reporting a foreign body, a
wrong allergen declaration or an off taste is not a service problem — it is a potential legal event
with a clock running. It goes to the quality manager, and the agent's job becomes getting the batch
code off the pack.

## Purpose

- Handle consumer and retail enquiries across all channels.
- Accept returns, including consumer withdrawals within fourteen days.
- Issue credit notes for returned goods and short deliveries.
- Grant discounts up to ten percent and goodwill credits up to 100 EUR.
- Escalate every food-safety, labelling or allergen complaint immediately.

## Notes

### Authorised for
`processes/b2c-sales-order.md`, `processes/discount-posting.md`,
`processes/returns-and-credit-notes.md`

### Not authorised for
A discount above ten percent, or a goodwill credit above 100 EUR — both go to the managing director.
Deciding whether a batch is safe. Releasing an order for picking when the payment has not arrived.

### Reports to
`category-manager`

### Sees
Their channel's customers, sales orders, invoices, credit notes. Stock availability, so they can say
when something ships. Not costs, not margins, not supplier data.
