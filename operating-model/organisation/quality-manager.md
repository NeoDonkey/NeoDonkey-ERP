# Quality manager

The person who decides whether food may be sold. A smaller job description than most quality roles
get, and a much larger authority. Both are deliberate.

Every batch of a quality-critical article arrives quarantined and leaves only when this role has
inspected it, checked the documents, and recorded a decision with their signing key on it. Nobody can
overrule that decision — not warehouse management, not the managing director. A company where
commercial pressure can release a batch is a company that will eventually release the wrong one.

The role's other half is escalation. An allergen complaint, a foreign body, a deviation at a critical
control point: these reach the quality manager immediately, because notifying the authorities under
EU 178/2002 is a decision taken in hours, not at the next weekly round.

## Purpose

- Inspect and release, restrict or block every incoming batch of a quality-critical article.
- Keep a retained sample for every released batch.
- Own the HACCP plan, the critical control points, and the records that prove they were monitored.
- Investigate quality complaints, connect them to a batch, and decide whether a withdrawal is needed.
- Decide whether returned goods may re-enter sellable stock. Usually they may not.
- Verify supplier food-safety certifications and raise findings in the quarterly supplier review.

## Notes

### Authorised for
`processes/quality-inspection.md`, `processes/stock-write-off.md`

### Not authorised for
Posting goods receipts — separating receiving from releasing is the point. Anything commercial:
prices, discounts, orders, invoices. Approving the write-off they themselves initiated at or above
500 EUR.

### Reports to
`managing-director`

Deliberately direct. A quality function reporting into operations reports into the pressure it exists
to resist.

### Sees
All batches, all inspections, all goods receipts, across every location. Supplier certifications. Not
customer balances, not margins.
